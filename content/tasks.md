TrialDesignBench tasks are organized around the parts of clinical trial design where agentic systems must integrate evidence, constraints, and structured outputs.

## Task Families

- Protocol concept development.
- Eligibility criteria and safety exclusions.
- Endpoint selection and estimand framing.
- Sample size and operating characteristic reasoning.
- Adaptive and group sequential design choices.
- Operational feasibility and site constraints.
- Risk review and protocol documentation.

## Example Task Template

Each task page can include the clinical scenario, input materials, agent instructions, expected artifacts, grading checkpoints, and notes for reproducibility.
