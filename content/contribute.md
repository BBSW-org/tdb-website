TrialDesignBench welcomes contributions from clinicians, statisticians, trialists, benchmark developers, and AI agent researchers.

## Contribution Areas

- Realistic task scenarios.
- Expert grading rubrics.
- Statistical and clinical validation checks.
- Evaluation harness improvements.
- Documentation, examples, and reproducibility notes.

## Contact

Open an issue on GitHub to discuss task ideas, evaluation design, or website updates.
